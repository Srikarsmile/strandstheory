Our Story

Built for Women Who've Always Deserved More

Strands Theory was born from a gap we lived every day — as women of colour, we knew the struggle of walking into salons that didn't understand our texture, our needs, or our vision. We created the space we wished had always existed.

Whether you're rocking your natural texture, protecting your hair while switching up your look, or treating yourself to the length and volume you've been dreaming of — we're here because we get it. Your hair journey is personal, and it deserves someone who truly understands it.
