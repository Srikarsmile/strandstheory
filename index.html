Our Techniques

Three Signature Methods

Every head of hair is unique. That's why we offer three precision installation techniques — each designed for different hair types, lifestyles, and looks.

F Tips

Flat Tip Extensions

Ultra-flat bonds that sit flush against the scalp for a completely invisible finish. Perfect for fine to medium hair — lightweight, comfortable, and undetectable even in updos and high ponytails.

  • Flat, discreet bond
  • Ideal for fine hair
  • Comfortable for updos
Most Popular

K Tips

Keratin Tip Extensions

The gold standard of hair extensions. Pre-bonded keratin tips are fused to your natural hair using gentle heat, creating a strong, long-lasting bond that moves and flows naturally for months.

  • Keratin heat-fused bond
  • Long-lasting (3-6 months)
  • Natural movement & flow

I Tips

Micro-Bead Extensions

No heat, no glue — just tiny micro-beads that clamp individual strands into place. The gentlest method available, ideal for those who want a damage-free installation that's easy to maintain and reposition.

  • No heat or glue needed
  • Gentle on natural hair
  • Easy to adjust & reuse
